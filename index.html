The shop on Belair Road

Angelo runs this place the old way. You talk to him, he looks at the car, and he tells you what it needs, not what makes the biggest ticket. Customers write about him welding a repair on the spot for a family just passing through, cleaning a gummed-up valve instead of quoting a rebuild, and beating the dealership's price outright.

He's been on Belair Road since 2014, and the shop has been named one of the top three towing companies in Baltimore by ThreeBestRated. Two flatbeds sit ready around the clock, and the man who answers at 3 p.m. is the man who answers at 3 a.m.

When you pull up, go around back. The storefront faces the road; the bays and the tow yard are behind it.
